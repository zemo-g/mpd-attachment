### 2. The exit chokes by numerical cooling, not by acceleration

Exit column, mass-flux-weighted, i = 98..104: p 737 -> 651, T 9700 ->
9115 K, v_z 2045 -> 2090, M 0.96 -> 1.07, smooth in the core. The
outer annulus is not: at r = 5.98 / 6.28 / 6.38 cm the last three cells
read T 216/158/60, 119/82/31, 115/61/30 K and p 75/54/21, 49/34/13,
47/25/12 Pa. 21 exit cells are subsonic on the frozen c_s; 13 cells
carry 8.7% of mdot at T < 300 K (below the injection and wall
temperatures). The LxF diffusive energy flux through the exit face is
16.1 kW (9.5% of P_ohm). Mechanism: the subsonic branch of
mv_ghost_exit sets the ghost's e_int to amb_p/(gamma-1) = 1.5 J/m^3
against 100-1000 J/m^3 in the cell; the first-order LxF face then
removes (en_c - en_g)/4 per step, i.e. 25% of the cell's thermal energy
per step, until c_s < v_z flips the cell to the zero-gradient branch.
The cell "chokes" by cooling. The sonic line is pinned at the exit
plane everywhere: axis M 0.84 at z 12.4 -> 1.04 at 12.8; r = 3 cm M
0.88 at 11.2 -> 1.04 at 12.2. p_exit 12 Pa (log) is the cooled sheet.

Consequences: u_exit = c_s(T_exit) is a boundary number; the 6.2 N exit
pressure term, M_exit and p_exit are scheme numbers; the 09-03 verdict
"exit choked, so the floor is physics" rests on this face.

Fix (proposal): a characteristic subsonic outlet (ghost from the
outgoing Riemann invariants + amb_p) with NO diffusive term on the exit
face, or a local-speed (Rusanov) dissipation at first-order faces:
dz/4dt is 28.8 km/s against a local signal speed of ~3 km/s at the
exit. Selftest: a 1D constant-area subsonic outflow at fixed M against
the exact state; one step must change the exit cell's e_int by O(dt),
not by 25%.

### 3. First-order LxF at solid faces: penalties, pumping, corners

Every face touching a non-fluid cell is first order, with the full
cell/ghost jump under dx/4dt = 11.5 km/s (r) and 28.8 km/s (z), set by
the global vmax (13,900 m/s, Alfven at the tip), not the local speed.
- Wall penalty on normal momentum: the mirror ghost makes the diffusive
  term (dz/2dt) rho v_n dA, a force that is not a pressure. It carries
  -4.75 N of the wall force (anode inner -1.39, anode outer -2.81,
  backplate -1.03, tip -0.05, corners +1.26 net) at mean v_n of 24 / 56
  / 0.7 / 18 m/s in the adjacent cells. The wall-adjacent pressures
  (what compare_bp_profile reads at the backplate) are under-read by
  the same amount: ~130 Pa over the backplate wall area.
- Magnetic pumping at the free cathode: the barrel ghost B_f r_f/r_g is
  5.4% above the cell, and the r-face diffusive term moves 0.518 T m^3/s
  of B and 60.7 kW of magnetic energy INTO the barrel cells every
  second (36% of P_ohm, gross). The energy closure says the implicit
  step returns most of it (unbooked), so the net is a bias of the
  near-cathode B profile, i.e. of the attachment itself, of unknown
  size. The numerical magnetic diffusivity dx^2/4dt is 5.8 (r) / 36 (z)
  m^2/s against eta/mu0 of 48 (3 eV) to 250 (1 eV) in the arc: 15-75%
  of the physical value in z at the tip corner, where the attachment
  peak sits.
- Corner ghosts serve two faces with one state: the anode corners
  exchange 2.3e-4 kg/s each way (4% of mdot, net +7e-6), the cathode
  corner leaks 8.5e-5 (1.4%), with matching momentum (+1.26 N) and
  energy (+0.8 / -2.1 kW) terms.

### 4. Parallel Spitzer resistivity in a perpendicular arc, Hall off

mv_eta_calc uses eta_par = 5.2e-5 lnL / T_eV^1.5. j lies in the r-z
plane and B is azimuthal, so j is perpendicular to B everywhere;
Braginskii's transverse resistivity rises from eta_par at x -> 0 to
1.96 eta_par at x = omega_ce tau_e >> 1. With NRL tau_e (electron-
neutral collisions included at the same sigma_en), x > 1 in 72% of
the fluid volume and 34% of the Ohmic dissipation sits at x > 1 (69%
at x > 0.2); the 20 hottest-dissipation cells span x 0.15-2.1. The
same x says the Hall term (run_hall = 0.0 in every run) is not small
in the anode region and the reservoir, which is where program.md says
it drives onset. sigma_en = 1e-19 m^2 is the 5-10 eV argon value; the
momentum-transfer cross-section at 1 eV is ~1.5e-20 (Ramsauer), so
eta_en is 5-7x high in the 0.7-1.5 eV band where "constriction
emerges". Fix: eta_perp(x) from Braginskii (n_e, T, lnL, B are all in
the cell; ~5 lines), selftest at the x -> 0 and x -> inf limits;
sigma_en(T) table; Hall smoke once the implicit Hall (P2 in the header)
exists, since the whistler dt is ~100x.
